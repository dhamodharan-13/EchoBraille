SIH 2026 Problem Statement & Architecture Specs

Hardware feasibility, cost analysis, and system architecture for Team EchoBraille (PS ID: SIH26215).

1. Problem Statement

AI communicates primarily through text or voice, which remains inaccessible to deafblind users. Over 1.5 Billion people have hearing loss and 2.2 Billion have vision impairment worldwide. Existing commercial electronic Braille displays are extremely expensive (₹1.5 Lakh to ₹4 Lakhs). EchoBraille provides a portable, affordable AI-to-Braille system for independent access through touch.

2. Hardware Architecture & Feasibility

  • ESP32 Microcontroller: Dual-core LX6, built-in Wi-Fi & Bluetooth BLE for low-latency wireless communication.
  • PCA9685 I2C Driver: 16-channel 12-bit PWM driver providing smooth independent micro-servo motor control.
  • 6 Servo-Driven Pins: Micro SG90 / linear servos creating physical elevated tactile dots.
  • SH1106 OLED Display: 1.3-inch 128x64 display providing visual text confirmation for educators and companions.
  • Target Cost: ≈ ₹1,500 per hardware module compared to traditional displays.

3. Future Scope & Roadmap

  • Multi-Character Display: Upgrading from single-cell refreshable output to multi-cell Braille strip.
  • Electromagnetic Actuation: Transitioning to micro-solenoids or piezoelectric actuators for faster refresh rates (< 100ms).
  • Multilingual Support: Grade 2 contraction Braille & regional Indian language Braille conversion (Hindi, Tamil, Marathi, etc.).
