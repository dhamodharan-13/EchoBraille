SIH 2026 Specifications

Problem Statement (SIH26215)

1.5B+ people worldwide live with hearing loss and 2.2B+ with vision impairment. Existing refreshable Braille hardware costs ₹1.5L to ₹4L. EchoBraille bridges conversational AI and the deafblind community via an open-standard ₹1,500 6-dot actuator.

Hardware BOM (≈ ₹1,500 INR)

  • ESP32 Microcontroller: ₹380 ($4.50)
  • PCA9685 16-Channel 12-Bit PWM Driver: ₹180 ($2.15)
  • 6x Micro SG90 9g Servos: ₹720 ($8.60)
  • 0.96" SSD1306 / SH1106 OLED: ₹160 ($1.90)
  • 3D Printed Tactile Braille Cap: ₹60 ($0.70)
